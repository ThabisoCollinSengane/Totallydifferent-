# Hair grades and starter range for Totallydifferent

Checked: 2026-11-20. Prices in ZAR unless stated. "Unverified" = not confirmed from a source I actually saw; treat as a planning guess.

## Bottom line

- Start with mid-grade **remy (cuticle-aligned) human hair**, not "raw". Raw costs roughly 2-3x more per bundle and the word is unregulated and often misused, so a small shop pays a premium it cannot prove to customers.
- Lead with **ready-made 4x4 closure bob wigs** plus a few straight bundles and one closure. Bobs use little hair, so the cash tied up per sale is small.
- The real margin is in **accessories** (melting spray, edge control, caps, bonnets, stands). Sell them as kits with the wig. Bundles and wigs alone look thin once duty and VAT are added.
- Imported bob wigs at about US$30 land at about R840, and local retail listings I saw were as low as R779. Price on quality and kits, not on being cheapest. Confirm duty, VAT and clearing fees with an agent before paying any supplier.
- Never write "raw", "virgin" or an origin on a label unless the supplier gives written proof. See the legal section.

## 1. Hair grades in plain words

### Comparison table: raw vs virgin vs remy vs non-remy

The owner's forgotten term is most likely **remy** (or **non-remy**). Those four words describe different things, which is why they overlap.

| Term | What it describes | What it means | Typical life (vendor claim) | Typical price per bundle, US$ (vendor claim) | Catch |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Raw** | Source and handling | Unprocessed, cuticles intact and aligned, supposedly from one donor | 1-3 years | 140-200+ | Not a regulated term, often misused. Definitions differ between sellers. |
| **Virgin** | Chemical history | Never dyed, bleached, permed or relaxed. Cuticles may not be aligned | 4-8 months (non-remy virgin); 6-12 months (virgin remy) | 60-100 (non-remy); 75-140 (remy) | Says nothing about cuticle direction, so it can still tangle. |
| **Remy** | Cuticle direction | Cuticles kept intact and all facing the same way, so it tangles less | 6-12 months (processed remy) | 50-90 | Remy can be virgin or processed. Not all remy is virgin. |
| **Non-remy** | Cuticle direction | Cuticles stripped or mixed, usually chemically processed, from many donors | 2-4 months | 20-50 | Cheapest. Tangles and matts fastest. |

Sources: lifespan and price rows are from the [Alibaba Seller Blog remy/virgin/raw guide](https://seller.alibaba.com/blogs/2026/southeast-asia/beauty-human-hair/remy-virgin-raw-hair-guide-alibaba-b2b) (read 2026-11-20). The raw "1-3 years" claim is repeated by other vendors. This is seller marketing, so treat the numbers as indicative. Definitions agree with the South African retailer explainer [Hair Majesty](https://www.hairmajesty.co.za/blogs/the-queen-z-scoop/the-difference-between-raw-virgin-hair-remy-hair-and-non-remy-hair) and a [Hermosa Hair comparison](https://www.hermosahair.com/blogs/blog/raw-hair-vs-virgin-hair-vs-remy-hair-vs-non-remy-hair) (search results only, not fully read). One vendor says raw hair comes from 2-5 donors rather than one, so even "raw" is not standard.

Rule of thumb: remy = cuticle direction, virgin = no chemicals, raw = a loose premium label. Good hair can be both remy and virgin.

### How to test a sample before a big order

1. **Burn test.** A few strands, tweezers, flame, in a ventilated place. Human hair burns slowly, smells like singed hair and leaves soft crushable ash. Synthetic melts, balls up, smells chemical and leaves a hard bead ([vendor guide](https://eayonhair.com/blogs/blog/how-to-test-human-hair-vs-synthetic-hair-a-simple-guide), [other vendor guide](https://nichair.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/151000145572-how-to-tell-the-difference-between-human-hair-and-synthetic-hair-), search results). Not foolproof: it will not tell you remy from non-remy or virgin from processed.
2. **Wash test.** Shampoo a sample bundle. A vendor guide says water stays clear for good raw human hair and darkens for synthetic or dyed hair ([BGM Girl](https://www.bgmgirl.com/blog/how-to-test-if-a-bundle-is-raw-human-hair/), search result only). Also check for tangling, a chemical smell and colour change after drying.
3. **Shedding test.** No source found, so mark unverified. Practical method: run fingers or a comb through the bundle several times, then shake it and weigh or count the hair that comes out of the weft. Heavy shedding means a weak weft or short strands.
4. **Cuticle check (remy).** Run your fingers down the hair (smooth) and up it (resistance). That is the usual tell for aligned cuticles. Unverified from a source in this run.
5. Buy a **sample first** (one bundle or one wig) from each of 2-3 suppliers before committing.

### How this may be marketed in South Africa

- The Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 (CPA) section 41(1)(a) bars a supplier from expressing or implying a false, misleading or deceptive representation about a material fact, by words or conduct. Section 41(1)(c) bars failing to correct a customer's apparent misunderstanding. Section 29(a) bars marketing goods in a way likely to imply a false or misleading representation, as contemplated in section 41. Sources: [Acts Online, section 41](https://www.acts.co.za/consumer-protection/41_false_misleading_or_deceptive_representations), [Acts Online, section 29](https://www.acts.co.za/consumer-protection/29_general_standards_for_marketing_of_goods_or_services), and a [law-firm commentary summary](https://www.polity.org.za/article/the-consumer-protection-act-and-its-impact-on-advertising-and-marketing-in-south-africa-2025-02-28). Checked 2026-11-20 from search summaries; the statute pages were not fully read.
- A true statement can still be misleading. Commentary notes this too.
- I found **no South African rule that defines "raw", "virgin", "remy" or hair origin** (unverified, absence of evidence only). So the test is whether the claim is true and provable. Calling mixed or processed hair "raw" or "virgin", or non-Vietnamese hair "Vietnamese", is exposure under section 41.
- Safe practice: (a) get written supplier specs per batch; (b) keep photos and invoices; (c) describe hair as "100% human hair, remy" only if confirmed; (d) call blends "human hair blend" and synthetic "synthetic"; (e) write "Brazilian-style" or "Brazilian texture" unless origin is proven; (f) state returns terms. Have a South African attorney check wording before launch.

## 2. Starter range recommendation

Rank for a very small budget and low margin:

1. **Pre-made bob wigs (4x4 closure, 10-12 inch, straight).** Little hair per unit, fast to sell, easy to photograph. Local listings seen: R779 for a 12" Brazilian glueless full-frontal bob ([PriceCheck/Takealot](https://www.pricecheck.co.za/offers/244862005/12IN%2BBrazilian%2BGlueless%2BMaroon%2BFull%2BFrontal%2BStraight%2BBob%2BWig)), R1,692 (reduced from R1,990) for a Brazilian fringe bob kinky curl at [Hair Majesty](https://www.hairmajesty.co.za/products/brazilian-kinky-curl-fringe-full-cap). Wide spread, so there is room to position at about R1,100-1,400 with a quality story (this price is my judgement, not a sourced figure).
2. **Straight 100g bundles, 12-18 inch, mid-grade remy.** Local snapshots: Brazilian 10A straight 12" from R334.90 at Cosmetic Connection ([PriceCheck](https://www.pricecheck.co.za/offers/241072629/Brazilian+10A+Bundle+Straight+12)); 4 bundles 200g at R559 ([PriceCheck](https://www.pricecheck.co.za/offers/244716592/Brazilian%2B8%2B10%2B12%2BInch%2BStraight%2BHair%2BBundles%2B4%2BBundles%2B200G%2BHair%2BWeft)). Margins are thin, so keep the quantity small.
3. **One or two closures** to go with bundles (frontals later).
4. **Skip for now:** Vietnamese raw (MOQ 20-50 per spec per the guide above, and top grade), full-length 24"+ wigs, curly and coloured textures (more stock lines for the same cash), raw-grade anything.

## 3. Assumptions: exchange rate, duty, VAT

- **Exchange rate:** mid-market about R16.76 per US$1 (OFX, page stamped 13 Nov 2026, [OFX](https://www.ofx.com/en-us/exchange-rates/zar)); other sources showed R16.37 to R16.93 in the same fortnight (search results: Currency.wiki 10 Nov, Trustnet 11 Nov, The Right Trader 16 Nov). **Planning rate used: R17.00** to cover a payment margin. Checked 2026-11-20.
- **Duty:** 20% general rate for HS 6704.20 (wigs, hair-goods of human hair). Source: third-party freight data site [jlog](https://app.jlog.co.za/hs/6704-20-wigs-false-beards-eyebrows-and-eyelashes-switches-and-the-like-of-human-or-anima), not the official SARS tariff book, so **confirm with a clearing agent**. The same page lists lower EU/UK and SADC rates with a certificate of origin, which will not apply to Asian goods. Whether loose bundles also fall in 6704.20 or elsewhere under 6704 is unverified, and accessories (caps, sprays, stands, bonnets) will have other HS codes and rates (unverified).
- **VAT:** 15% assumed on import (value plus duty; SARS usually adds a customs uplift to the value, unverified). If you are not VAT-registered, you cannot claim it back, so it is a cost.
- **Freight and clearing:** I used a **freight uplift of 20% (small order) or 12% (R20k order)** on goods value and no separate clearing fee. Both are guesses (unverified). Get quotes from a courier or agent first. Small express parcels may attract different duty and VAT handling.
- **Landed multiplier** = (1 + freight) x 1.20 duty x 1.15 VAT = **1.65 for R5k** and **1.55 for R20k**.
