# SA top-selling hair categories - brief (checked 2026-11-20)

## Bottom line
- No source I could reach publishes actual unit-sales rankings for SA hair. Takealot/Clicks best-seller sorts did not render, and I found no SA media or Google Trends data. The ranking below is a proxy: the order of Hair City and Hair Majesty "best-selling" collection pages, plus an AliExpress-from-SA order-count ranking. Treat it as directional.
- Ready-to-wear lace wigs (4x4 / 13x4, Brazilian, straight and water wave) dominate every list I saw. Short bob/fringe/pixie wigs are the clear second.
- Entry price points: braiding hair at about R45-R95 a pack, single Brazilian bundles from about R299, and bob/pixie wigs from about R479-R1,249. That is where a startup can start with little stock.
- Clip-ins, ponytails and Vietnamese/Peruvian appear as listings only. I found no evidence they are top sellers, so they are runners-up.
- Seasonality (Dec/Jan) is unverified. I found no SA source. See the note below.

## Low price points for a startup
- Entry items proven to sell at low prices: braiding hair (R45-R95), single bundles (R299-R549), pixie full-cap wigs (R479), and a lace-glue/edge-tamer accessory attach (R89-R149; several were sold out at Hair Majesty, which suggests demand).
- Competitors push lace wigs to R999-R1,299 on sale, and 2-for-1 combos at R1,799-R2,499. Undercutting needs a landed cost well below that.
- A small startup can enter with a short, tight range: 8-12" bob/pixie wigs, 4x4 straight/water wave, and a few bundles. Avoid premium Peruvian/Vietnamese until demand is proven.
- Accessories (glue, melting spray, travel bag R149) raise basket size.

## Seasonality (unverified)
- I found no SA source for December festive demand or January back-to-school demand, so both are unverified.
- Inference only: Dec festive demand is plausible for wigs and bundles, and Jan for low-maintenance styles and braiding. Hair City already runs "sale" and combo pricing, which suggests promo-driven demand.
- The owner could check by looking at the past-year Google Trends for "wigs" and "braiding hair" in South Africa (not retrieved).

## Gaps
- Takealot and Clicks best-seller sorts did not render. Sorbet and other beauty stores were not checked. No SA TikTok or media trend article with rankings was found.
- A Treendly wigs trend page appeared in results but was not opened.
- The AliExpress ranking dated 2026-03-01 is dropshipping-from-SA, not local retail, and its 7-day figures were inconsistent.
- Mixed customer reviews (lace sizing, quality) were reported on retailer pages.
