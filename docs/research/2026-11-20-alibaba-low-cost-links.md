## Bottom line
- ZSF Hair / Xuchang Zs Hair: I found NO Alibaba storefront in any search result (a search for "Xuchang Zs Hair alibaba.com" returned other Xuchang sellers, none ZSF). Do not assume it exists; the owner can type the name into Alibaba's supplier search.
- Alibaba pages mostly would not load for me (HTTP 404/410, empty or field-less pages). One product page opened (XYS bob, price only) and one more gave price tiers only (GS-hair bundle). Everything else is from search snippets = UNVERIFIED. Many results were category/showroom pages, not single-supplier pages; they are labelled as such. Several snippets lacked a URL for the specific listing, so I give none rather than guess.
- Cheap does not equal cheap once landed. At one-off parcel sizes, landed cost is roughly 1.6-1.9x the FOB price (freight and clearing are fixed costs). A US$28 bundle lands at about R860-R950 versus SA retail R314-549; a US$20 closure lands at about R620-R680 versus R539-599. Bundles and closures do not make margin unless FOB is roughly US$14 or less, which is blend/synthetic territory. Only bob wigs plausibly leave room, and only if the cheap human-hair listings (US$18-45) pass a burn test.
- Recommendation: spend about R1,500-R2,000 on 3-4 SAMPLES (one bob wig, one bundle, one closure/frontal, one braid pack) via Trade Assurance before committing the R5,000. Do not buy 1-piece units to resell; negotiate 5-10 pieces from the one supplier whose sample passes.
- FX: US$1 = R17.55 (Wise converter page fetched 2026-11-20, page shows no timestamp; https://wise.com/us/currency-converter/usd-to-zar-rate). The earlier brief noted other undated snippets at 16.0-16.8, so ZAR may be 5-10% high. Re-check before paying.

Also: titles like "Brazilian" on listings whose text says Indian (row 8) mean the label is unreliable.

What to ask for: (1) a video of the burn test on THIS batch (human hair burns to powdery ash and smells like burnt hair; synthetic melts into a hard bead); (2) weight in grams per bundle and wig; (3) hair grade and origin in writing; (4) a sample before bulk; (5) a written refund clause for blend hair, paid through Trade Assurance. A full-lace human-hair wig contains far more than $15-30 of hair, so under about US$40 expect blend unless proven.

## Legal and risk notes
- Consumer Protection Act: selling blend hair as "100% human" is a misrepresentation; test every batch before listing.
- Counterfeit/IP: avoid listings using brand names or "private label" copying other brands (e.g. other vendors' names in titles, as in row 7's title).
- Import: you are the importer of record; duty and VAT are due on arrival. Get a clearing quote before paying. Do not allow undervalued customs declarations (a Vietnamese vendor in the earlier brief was reported for this).
- Payment: Trade Assurance or card only; refuse bank transfer to personal accounts. Customer data (POPIA) is not involved at the sourcing stage.
