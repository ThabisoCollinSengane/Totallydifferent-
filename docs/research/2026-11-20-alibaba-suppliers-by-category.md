## Bottom line
- No supplier here is proven safe. Only ZSF Hair (4.8, 171 reviews), Unihairvn (4.7, 48) and Megalook (4.5, 1,317) have meaningful independent Trustpilot evidence; the Xuchang "known leads" (Weiqi, Alice, Taimeite, Faqi, Nanming) have NO independent evidence that I could find. Mark them "no independent evidence".
- Alibaba storefront and product pages did not load for me (blank content on every Alibaba page I tried), so Alibaba MOQs/prices below come from search snippets only and are unverified until the owner opens the listing.
- Best first contacts for a ~R5,000 (about US$285) first order: ZSF Hair (wigs, bundles, frontals, own site, 30-day return claimed) and Megalook (bob/lace wigs; but recent Nov 2026 reviews allege synthetic blend). Order ONE sample each, do not split R5,000 across many suppliers.
- Do not pay by bank transfer. Use Alibaba Trade Assurance / card, and run a burn test and shedding test on every sample.
- FX rate used: US$1 = R17.56 (Wise mid-market page fetched 2026-11-20, page showed no timestamp: https://wise.com/us/currency-converter/usd-to-zar-rate). Other undated snippets showed 16.0-16.8, so ZAR figures may be 5-10% too high. Re-check before paying. Shipping, duty and 15% VAT are NOT included.

## Suggested first-order plan (about R5,000 / US$285)
- 1 body-wave bundle + 1 lace frontal/closure + 1 bob wig from ZSF (or wholesale quote), 1 bob wig sample from Megalook, 1 straight Vietnamese sample from Unihairvn, 2 braid colours from Harmony. Likely exceeds budget at site prices; ask for wholesale quotes first.
- Test on arrival: burn test (human hair smells like burnt hair, ash crumbles; synthetic melts to a hard bead), wash-and-comb shedding test, lace/knot inspection. Film the unboxing for any dispute.
- Budget R300-R900 per parcel for DHL/courier plus duty and VAT: unverified estimate, get a quote.

## Questions to send each supplier (copy/paste)
1. Is the hair 100% human (not blend)? Which grade (raw, virgin, remy)? Can you send a burn-test video of this exact batch?
2. What is the MOQ for 1 wig / 1 bundle / 1 frontal? What is the price per piece at 1, 3, 10 pieces (USD, FOB and delivered to Durban/Johannesburg, South Africa)?
3. Do you sell samples? Price, is it refunded against a bulk order, and how many days to ship?
4. Which lace (HD/transparent/Swiss), density (150/180%), pre-plucked, bleached knots? Please send real photos/videos of the exact item, not catalogue images.
5. Production and delivery lead time, shipping method, tracking, and who declares customs value (I need the true value declared).
6. What is your refund/return policy for wrong colour, wrong length, shedding, blend hair? Who pays return shipping?
7. Payment: do you accept Alibaba Trade Assurance / card? (Refuse bank transfer to a personal account.)
8. Please send your business licence, factory address and 2 customer references in South Africa or Africa.
